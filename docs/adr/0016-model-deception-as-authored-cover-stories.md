# Model deception as authored Cover Stories and remembered Testimony

A Character may lie in authored or Knowledge-Driven Dialogue only by expressing
an Author-declared Cover Story; a Dialogue Provider may verbalise that Claim but
cannot invent a factual lie. A successful lie records Testimony for its speaker
and listener instead of adding the false Claim to Character Knowledge, allowing
the listener to remember and later contradict what was said without requiring
general beliefs, confidence, gossip propagation, or generated text to become
narrative authority.
