Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: full-body game character master for a 2D point-and-click adventure

Primary request: Raffaele, an elderly harbour keeper from Capri in 1535,
standing in a neutral idle pose

Subject: full body visible from head to shoes; weathered Mediterranean man
about 58 years old; lean practical build; sun-lined face; short greying hair;
off-white linen shirt with rolled sleeves; dark brown sleeveless wool
waistcoat; faded blue-grey knee-length breeches; worn brown leather shoes;

Style/medium: hand-painted early-1990s VGA adventure-game character art;

legible when reduced to about 70 pixels tall

Composition/framing: one single isolated character, front three-quarter view,

background removal

Lighting/mood: warm Mediterranean late-afternoon light matching a golden-hour
harbour scene; dry, pragmatic personality

Constraints: historically plausible Capri 1535 work clothing; background must
be exactly one uniform `#00ff00` with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor
plane, reflections, lighting variation or horizon; crisp edges; no cast shadow;
no contact shadow; do not use `#00ff00` anywhere in character; no text; no
watermark; no frame; no additional poses or objects

photorealism, anime, exaggerated caricature
