Use case: stylized-concept

harbour scene; dry, pragmatic personality

Constraints: historically plausible Capri 1535 work clothing; background must

no contact shadow; do not use `#00ff00` anywhere in character; no text; no
watermark; no frame; no additional poses or objects

photorealism, anime, exaggerated caricature
