# Michele Art Master prompts

All generated sources used the built-in image generation tool in
`stylized-concept` or `identity-preserve` mode. The old Michele sprites were
explicitly excluded as references. `turnaround-chroma.png` became the identity,
costume, proportion and palette reference for every Animation sheet.

## Turnaround

Create the definitive orthographic turnaround for Michele, a 23-year-old native
Caprese maritime day worker in 1535. Show left-facing profile, front,
right-facing profile and back as equal full-body views. Preserve a lean
work-conditioned build, youthful Mediterranean face, dark wavy hair, stubble,
ecru rolled-sleeve shirt, deep-petrol sleeveless doublet, terracotta sash,
brown-olive knee breeches, bare lower legs and low leather shoes. His leather
pouch and working cord belong on the anatomical left hip: visible in `left`,
hidden in `right`, image-right in `front`, and image-left in `back`. Use original
hand-painted illustrated neo-retro graphic-adventure art with simplified chunky
forms, grouped coloured shadows and crisp antialiased edges. Use a perfectly
flat uniform `#ff00ff` chroma-key background without shadow, floor, gradient,
texture, text or watermark. Avoid pirate, soldier, noble, fantasy, heroic,
photorealistic, anime and pixel-art cues.

## Idle

Create a regular two-row by three-column key-pose sheet. The paired rows own the
requested Facings. Keep the pose, arms, hands, feet, head direction and Ground
Point fixed. Only a nearly imperceptible chest breath may change between poses;
there is no gesture, weight shift, horizon check or whole-body bobbing. Preserve
the turnaround identity, construction, lighting and anatomical pouch rule.

## Speaking

Create a regular two-row by four-column neutral speaking loop. Pose order is a
closed mouth, speech onset, open mouth, then closing mouth. Keep both arms,
hands, feet, torso width and Ground Point completely fixed. The front row must
show a clear closed/open/closing mouth cycle; the back row uses only a minute
head motion and never reveals the face. Preserve identity and the anatomical
pouch rule. No hand gesture is permitted in the speaking loop.

## Walking

Create a regular two-row by four-column economical walk cycle. Pose order is
contact A, passing A, contact B with the opposite leg, and passing B. Michele has
a slight forward-leading torso, contained arm swing and restrained sash, cord
and pouch secondary motion. Keep equal scale and a common contact baseline.
Author every Facing rather than mirroring it, preserving the anatomical pouch
rule.

## Use winch

Create a regular two-row by four-column finite action: planted preparation,
two-handed reach toward an implied waist-height mechanism, firm two-handed
contact and body-weight engagement, then controlled pull/turn completion. Do not
draw the mechanism. Keep the feet planted at one Ground Point and preserve the
anatomical pouch rule.

## Pick up

Create a regular two-row by four-column finite action: standing preparation,
knees and torso bending while the right hand reaches down, the right hand
closing at the implied ground contact point, then rising and returning the hand
toward the left-hip pouch. Draw no item, avoid a full squat, and preserve the
Ground Point and anatomical pouch rule.

Every sheet uses full RGB colour, the approved soft neutral Mediterranean light,
a perfectly uniform `#ff00ff` chroma-key field, and no cast/contact shadow,
labels, watermark, scenery or extra objects.
