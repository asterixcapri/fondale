# Michele turnaround v1

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: identity-lock Character turnaround Art Master for a 2D
point-and-click Game Project

Input images: the Capri alley and harbour Background Art Masters are style,
palette, lighting, camera-height, and final-scene references only. Do not copy
their composition and do not add scenery. No previous Michele Character image
is an input or reference.

Primary request: create one coherent full-body four-view turnaround of Michele,
the 23-year-old protagonist of a 2D adventure set on Capri in 1535. Show exactly
four separate views in this order from left to right: front, left profile, right
profile, back. All four views depict the same Character in the same relaxed,
stable standing pose and at exactly the same body height and Ground Point.

Subject: Michele is a young Mediterranean Caprese working man with sun-tanned
skin, a lean practical build earned through boats, ropes, steep paths, and
small manual jobs, and thick near-black brown hair that is slightly untidy. His
face is young, alert, curious, expressive, and quietly self-assured rather than
heroic. He is neither large nor overtly muscular.

Costume: historically plausible modest Capri clothing for 1535—an ivory linen
shirt with naturally rolled sleeves; a plain, fitted brown wool waistcoat; a
simple leather belt; blue-grey knee breeches; plain stockings leaving part of
the lower leg visible; robust brown ankle shoes. Keep garment construction,
seams, closures, folds, lengths, and colours identical in all views.

Asymmetry lock: Michele is right-handed. A small plain leather pouch is fixed
to his physical left hip. A modest working knife in a plain sheath is fixed to
his physical right hip. Preserve their true physical sides in every view;
never mirror or swap them. The knife is a practical rope-and-fishing tool, not
a weapon display.

Style/medium: original hand-painted early-1990s VGA adventure-game Character
illustration, matching the attached Capri Backgrounds through simplified
readable forms, clean pixel-art-inspired edges, restrained clustered painterly
dithering, and detail designed to survive reduction to a 100-pixel-tall Runtime
Asset. Avoid photorealism, modern digital concept-art rendering, anime, and
exaggerated caricature.

Composition/framing: one horizontal production sheet divided mentally into
four equal cells, with one and only one complete figure centred in each cell.
Full body from hair to shoe soles visible with generous padding. Identical
camera height, scale, proportions, head size, shoulder width, limb length, and
Ground Point in every cell. Stable neutral pose suitable as the first frame of
an animation. No overlap between figures.

Lighting/mood: warm Mediterranean golden-hour light from the upper left with
restrained violet-brown shadow, matching the Background references. Michele
looks practical, observant, and approachable, with the potential for dry comic
reactions.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background for local
background removal. The background must be exactly one uniform colour with no
shadows, gradient, texture, reflections, floor plane, horizon, or lighting
variation. Do not use `#00ff00` anywhere in the Character.

Constraints: exactly four figures and exactly the requested view order; same
identity, costume, scale, light, and palette in all four views; no cast or
contact shadow; no labels, letters, numbers, grid lines, borders, scenery,
props beyond the worn pouch and sheathed working knife, motion blur, glow,
watermark, or extra poses.

Avoid: Caribbean pirate styling, bandana, feathered hat, tricorn, large sabre,
firearm, armour, noble clothing, soldier uniform, fantasy elements, modern
clothing, bodybuilder proportions, heroic power pose, theatrical swashbuckler
stance, or independent redesigns between views.
