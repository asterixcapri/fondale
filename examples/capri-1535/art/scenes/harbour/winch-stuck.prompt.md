# Stuck winch master

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: interactive Scenery master for a 2D point-and-click adventure

Primary request: a small sixteenth-century wooden harbour winch in a stuck,
neglected state, used to haul a fishing boat onto shore

Subject: compact horizontal wooden drum between two low heavy timber supports;
dark hand-forged iron axle and bands; coarse rope wound around the drum and
trailing only a short distance; empty square socket clearly visible on the
right side where a removable hand crank should fit; dry, salt-worn, slightly
crooked but intact; strong readable silhouette

Style/medium: hand-painted early-1990s VGA adventure-game scenery art;
simplified readable construction; grouped pixel-like details; warm weathered
wood, dark iron and purple shadows; designed to remain legible around 55 pixels
wide and 42 pixels tall

Composition/framing: one single isolated winch, three-quarter view from
slightly above, centered, entire silhouette visible with generous padding;
ground-facing base aligned horizontally

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background for local
background removal

Lighting/mood: warm Mediterranean late-afternoon light from upper left
matching a Capri harbour

Constraints: historically plausible Capri 1535 hand-powered mechanism; no
crank installed; background must be exactly one uniform `#00ff00` with no
shadow, gradient, texture, floor plane, reflection or lighting variation;
crisp edges; no cast shadow; no contact shadow; do not use `#00ff00` in the
winch; no text; no watermark; no frame; no people; no extra tools

Avoid: modern gears exposed externally, motor, steel cable, polished
machinery, ship's wheel, fantasy, photorealism
