# Oil flask scale

The oil flask uses the accepted `master.png` Art Master to derive two separate
Runtime presentations: a `17×22` Scene asset anchored at its bottom centre and
a purpose-designed `32×32` Inventory Appearance. At its harbour Ground Point,
Perspective Scale displays the Scene asset below native size; it is never
enlarged.

`engine-scale-check.png` is the `1280×720` actual-play-size Engine diagnostic.
It shows the moved fishing nets in their final harbour composition and the oil
flask's Inventory Appearance at native resolution beside the sealed letter.
