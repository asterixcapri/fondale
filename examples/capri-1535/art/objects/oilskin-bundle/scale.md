# Oilskin bundle scale

The bundle uses the accepted `master.png` and `opened-master.png` Art Masters
to derive two Scene Appearances and one Inventory Appearance:

- `wrapped`: `56×36` Scene asset anchored at its bottom centre `(28, 36)`; at
  the handoff Ground Point `(950, 596)` the drifting-boat Perspective Scale
  displays it at `94%`, below native size; it is never enlarged.
- `opened`: `72×44` Scene asset anchored at its bottom centre `(36, 44)`; the
  wider union footprint of the unfolded cloth keeps the same Ground Point.
- Inventory: purpose-designed `32×32` icon at the project Inventory Appearance
  Size.

`engine-scale-check.png` is the `1280×720` actual-play-size Engine diagnostic.
It shows both Scene Appearances over the accepted drifting-boat composition
and the Inventory Appearance at native resolution.
